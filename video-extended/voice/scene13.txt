The labels make the data trainable. We trained a small policy on four hundred eighty scripted demonstrations, then fine-tuned one copy on the lifted ones and one on the crushed ones. The difference in weights is a task vector. Add the lifted vector, with no new training, and success on unseen eggs goes from twenty-seven to sixty-seven percent, while crushes fall from sixty-two to twenty-eight. Add the crushed vector, and crushes rise to eighty-three. Not everything works: subtracting the crushed vector breaks the reach. Scripted demonstrations, simulated egg.
